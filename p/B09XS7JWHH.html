The case for it

If you fly, commute, or work anywhere with other humans in it, this is the one to get. The cancellation is a genuine step above the XM4, the call quality finally stopped being embarrassing, and the 30-hour battery means you stop thinking about charging it.

What you actually get

  • Best-in-class active noise cancellation, especially on low-frequency drone
  • Around 30 hours per charge, 3 minutes of charging gets you roughly 3 hours
  • Multipoint pairing — laptop and phone connected at the same time
  • Speak-to-Chat pauses playback when you start talking

Where it falls down

They no longer fold flat, so the case is noticeably bulkier than the XM4's. If you live out of a small bag, that matters.

The case for it

The value case is the lifespan. Fans that never spin up, a battery that lasts a working day rather than a meeting, and enough headroom that it will still feel quick in year four. If you compile, render, or edit for a living it pays for itself.

What you actually get

  • Runs near-silent on workloads that make other laptops audible
  • Genuinely all-day battery, not marketing all-day
  • Mini-LED display with real HDR
  • HDMI, SD and MagSafe are back

Where it falls down

Expensive, and Apple's storage and memory upgrades are priced with contempt. Configure carefully at purchase — none of it is upgradeable later.

The case for it

It is expensive for a mouse and it is still the easiest recommendation on this list. The MagSpeed wheel alone changes how you move through long documents and spreadsheets, and the quiet switches make open-plan life better for everyone near you.

What you actually get

  • MagSpeed electromagnetic scrolling — ratchet for precision, free-spin for distance
  • Near-silent click switches
  • Pairs with three devices, switch between them with one button
  • USB-C, and a full day of use from a few minutes of charge

Where it falls down

Shaped firmly for right hands and medium-to-large ones at that. Small hands and left hands should look elsewhere.

The case for it

Clear cases are a commodity right up until the magnet ring matters. Cheap ones let a MagSafe charger slide off centre and charge slowly or not at all. This one aligns every time, which is worth the difference on its own.

What you actually get

  • Built-in magnet ring that aligns MagSafe chargers correctly
  • Raised edges around the camera bezel and screen
  • Scratch-resistant coating on both faces
  • Buttons stay clicky rather than mushy

Where it falls down

Every clear case yellows eventually and this one is no exception. Treat it as a consumable, not a purchase.

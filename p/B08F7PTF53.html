The case for it

Everything before this needed a gaming PC and a room full of sensors. This needs a coffee table's worth of space and nothing else, which is why it is the one that got used. Beat Saber alone justifies it for most households.

What you actually get

  • Fully standalone — no PC, no cables, no external sensors
  • Can also tether to a gaming PC for higher-end titles
  • Hand tracking without controllers for some apps
  • Guardian boundary keeps you from walking into furniture

Where it falls down

The stock head strap is uncomfortable past half an hour and most people end up buying a replacement. Motion sensitivity is real — try before committing.

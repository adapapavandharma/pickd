The case for it

The Find My network is the whole product — hundreds of millions of iPhones quietly reporting where your bag is. Nothing from any other brand comes close on coverage. Put one in checked luggage before you next fly and you will understand the appeal within one trip.

What you actually get

  • Precision Finding with on-screen direction and distance on recent iPhones
  • Piggybacks on the entire Find My network, not just your own devices
  • User-replaceable CR2032 battery, about a year per battery
  • IP67 water and dust resistant

Where it falls down

iPhone only — there is no real Android experience. And they have no keyring hole, so budget for a holder on top.

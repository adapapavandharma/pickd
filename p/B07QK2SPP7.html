The case for it

There is a whole category of speakers that sound slightly better indoors and die the first time they get wet. This is the other kind. It is the one to hand someone who wants music at a barbecue and will not be careful with it.

What you actually get

  • IPX7 waterproof — fully submersible, and it floats
  • Around 12 hours of playtime
  • PartyBoost pairs two of them for stereo
  • USB-C charging

Where it falls down

No 3.5mm input and no speakerphone, both of which the previous generation had. Bass is good for the size, which is still not a lot of bass.

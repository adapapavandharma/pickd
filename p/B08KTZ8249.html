The case for it

A Kindle is one of the few gadgets that reliably makes you do more of a good thing. The Paperwhite is the sweet spot in the range: the bigger screen and adjustable warm light are worth the step up from the base model, and you are not paying Oasis money for page-turn buttons.

What you actually get

  • 6.8" 300 ppi glare-free display that reads like paper in direct sun
  • Adjustable warm front light for night reading
  • IPX8 waterproof — survives a drop in the bath or the pool
  • Weeks of battery, not hours

Where it falls down

Lock-screen ads unless you pay to remove them, and it is still a walled garden — sideloading EPUBs means converting them first.

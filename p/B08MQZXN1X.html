The case for it

Televisions get slow long before their panels get bad. A streaming stick is the cheapest possible fix, and the Max is the version worth buying — the extra memory is the difference between an interface that responds and one that makes you wait.

What you actually get

  • 4K with Dolby Vision, HDR10+ and Dolby Atmos passthrough
  • Wi-Fi 6 for a steadier picture on a busy network
  • Meaningfully faster than the standard 4K stick
  • Remote controls TV power and volume too

Where it falls down

The home screen is an advertisement for Amazon's own content and there is no way to turn that off.

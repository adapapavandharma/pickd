The case for it

The Dot has quietly become a decent small speaker rather than a novelty. For a kitchen, a bedside table, or the room you want a timer and a weather report in, it does the job and costs very little.

What you actually get

  • Noticeably fuller sound than the 4th generation
  • Built-in temperature sensor for routines
  • Eero built in to extend a mesh network
  • Tap-to-snooze on the top

Where it falls down

It is an always-listening microphone made by a retailer. That is a fair trade for some people and a dealbreaker for others — decide deliberately.

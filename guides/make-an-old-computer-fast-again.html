Make an old computer feel new for under a hundred

Before you replace a four-year-old machine, spend twenty minutes and a fraction of the money. Most computers do not get slow — they get bottlenecked, and usually in the same two places.

Updated 3 September 2026 · 3 picks

Computers rarely die. They get slow enough that replacing them feels justified, and almost always for reasons that have nothing to do with the processor. The disk is the usual culprit, and the input devices are the usual reason the machine feels unpleasant even when it is fast.

This is the order to spend money in, cheapest and highest-impact first. If the first one does not fix it, the second probably will not either — at that point you have a genuine case for a new machine.

If the machine is running a spinning disk, or an early SATA SSD, this is the upgrade. Boot times go from a minute to a few seconds, applications open instead of thinking about it, and the general sense that the computer is arguing with you disappears.

It is twenty minutes of work, one screw on most laptops, and no cables. Nothing else on this list comes close on impact per pound.

Best for
Any machine three years old or more that still has a mechanical drive.
Skip it if
You move genuinely huge files every day. It is DRAM-less, so sustained large writes slow once the cache fills — irrelevant for normal use, noticeable for video work.

A faster disk fixes waiting. This fixes the hundreds of small frictions in between — the scroll wheel that free-spins through a thousand-row spreadsheet, the silent clicks, the three devices on one button.

It is a lot of money for a mouse and it remains the easiest recommendation here. People who buy it stop shopping for mice.

Best for
Anyone who spends hours a day in documents, spreadsheets or code.
Skip it if
You have small hands, or you are left-handed. It is shaped firmly for large right hands and no amount of wanting will change that.

The short version

The order matters more than the products. A great mouse on a machine that takes a minute to boot is lipstick; a fast disk on a machine you hate touching is only half a fix.

One thing this guide cannot sell you that often matters more than any of it: more memory. If the machine is swapping constantly, RAM beats everything here — and on most modern laptops it is soldered, which is the real reason old machines get replaced.

Questions people actually ask

Will an SSD help if the computer is only a year or two old?

Almost certainly not — anything recent already ships with NVMe storage. This upgrade is for machines with a mechanical drive or an early SATA SSD, which is mostly 2018 and earlier.

How do I know whether the disk is the bottleneck?

Open the task manager while the machine feels slow. If disk usage is pinned near 100% while the processor is mostly idle, you have your answer.

Four things that make travelling less bad

Not a packing list. Four specific objects that each remove one recurring annoyance from a trip, and nothing that only earns its space on the perfect journey.

Updated 3 September 2026 · 4 picks

Most travel gear lists are aspirational fiction — a packing cube system for a trip you will never take. This is the shorter, duller version: four things that each solve a problem you will definitely have.

The test for inclusion was simple. Would you notice it was missing?

The picks, in one line each

For the flight
Sony WH-1000XM5 Turns a six-hour flight into a shorter one. For the bag you checked
Apple AirTag (4-pack) The single best argument for the Find My network. For the day you cannot find a socket
Anker 737 Power Bank (PowerCore 24K) A second laptop battery that happens to charge phones too. For the waiting

For the flight

Sony WH-1000XM5

★★★★★★★★★★4.6 · 28,431 ratings
$348.00 Check price Full write-up →

Engine drone is exactly the kind of constant low-frequency noise that cancellation handles best, which is why headphones that are merely good at home feel transformative at altitude. You arrive less exhausted, which is the whole argument.

Best for
Long flights and open-plan hotel lobbies.
Skip it if
You are travelling carry-on only and counting every litre — the case is large.
For the bag you checked

Apple AirTag (4-pack)

★★★★★★★★★★4.7 · 141,203 ratings
$74.99 Check price Full write-up →

Put one in checked luggage before your next flight and you will understand within one trip. When a bag goes missing, the difference between 'the airline is looking into it' and 'it is at the airport you left four hours ago' is the difference between an afternoon lost and an afternoon fine.

The four-pack is the only sensible way to buy them — one for the case, one for the daypack, one for keys, one spare.

Best for
Checked bags, and anyone who has ever left a jacket somewhere.
Skip it if
You use Android. There is no real equivalent experience.
For the day you cannot find a socket

Anker 737 Power Bank (PowerCore 24K)

★★★★★★★★★★4.5 · 6,842 ratings
$109.99 Check price Full write-up →

Most power banks are a phone top-up dressed up as insurance. 140W of output means this one actually runs a laptop rather than slowing its decline, which is what you need on a long layover or a train with no working outlets.

The display showing real input and output wattage sounds like a gimmick until the first time it tells you the cable you are using is the slow one.

Best for
Long transit days and anyone who works while travelling.
Skip it if
You only need to top up a phone. It is a heavy brick and you would be carrying it for nothing.
For the waiting

Kindle Paperwhite

★★★★★★★★★★4.7 · 92,104 ratings

Travel is mostly waiting, and a Kindle is the most reliable way to convert waiting into something you are glad you did. Weeks of battery removes one more charger from the bag, and the front light means you can read on a night flight without waking the person beside you.

It is waterproof, which matters more by a pool than it sounds.

Best for
Anyone whose phone battery dies because they read on it.
Skip it if
You genuinely do not read. It will stay in the drawer, as every unread Kindle does.

The short version

If you only take one: the headphones, on any trip involving a plane. If the trip is mostly trains and hotels, the Kindle earns its place more often.

The power bank is the one to leave behind for a short trip. It is genuinely heavy, and on a two-day trip with reliable sockets it is dead weight you carry the whole way.

Questions people actually ask

Can you fly with a 24,000mAh power bank?

Yes — it sits under the 100Wh threshold airlines generally apply, so it travels in carry-on. Power banks must not go in checked luggage, which is a rule worth remembering the one time you are tempted.

Do trackers actually help when an airline loses a bag?

They change the conversation rather than the outcome. Knowing exactly where a bag is turns an open-ended claim into a specific request, and staff respond very differently to the second one.
